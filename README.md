This project explores Spotify listening history through an interactive Power BI dashboard, transforming listening activity into meaningful insights about music preferences, artist popularity within my listening history, and listening patterns.

The goal was to move beyond simply counting streams and use data visualization to understand what I listen to, which artists I return to, and how my listening habits can be explored through data.

**🔍 Project Objectives**

1. Transform raw listening history into an analysis-ready dataset.
2. Organize data into a structured model for efficient reporting.
3. Develop DAX measures to calculate listening KPIs.
4. Build interactive visuals to explore artists, tracks, and listening behaviour.
5. Present analytical findings through a clear, user-friendly dashboard.

**📊 Key Analytical Areas**

A. Listening Activity -Explore overall listening activity through measures such as total streams, total songs, and listening duration.
B. Artist Analysis-Identify frequently played artists and examine which artists account for a significant share of listening activity.
C. Listening Behaviour-Investigate repeated listening patterns to understand whether listening activity is concentrated around familiar artists or distributed across different artists.


**📈 Dashboard & User Experience**


I wanted the user who is visiting my dashboard to have the same feel as they see their Spotify App on their phone. A music-inspired presentation that connects the analytical experience with the Spotify theme


**💡 Business Value**

This project demonstrates how data analytics can turn activity-level records into a structured view of user behaviour.

The same analytical principles can be applied to business scenarios such as:

Customer engagement analysis

Product usage and repeat-usage behaviour

Content consumption patterns

Customer segmentation

KPI monitoring and performance reporting
